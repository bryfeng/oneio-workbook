02

Gateway V3 and what follows

The Flow adapter links ONE’s payment request to an approved merchant destination. Broader account and treasury services follow separately.

Proposed

Flow collection · SPARK first

Gateway + orchestration + Flow adapterResolve the merchant’s default custody or verified self-custody destination, create Flow, then reconcile custody credit or external-wallet receipt.

Business wallets · later

Account / wallet services + DynamicAdd shared ownership, team access and signer controls under ONE accounts, then expose verified wallet profiles to Gateway.

Treasury workflows · later

Payments, disbursements and TMS servicesScope crypto batch payouts, stablecoin card issuance and other treasury capabilities as independent product extensions.

Auto-conversion · on hold

Custody + trading + ledger + reconciliationKeep the earlier address, conversion and fiat-credit concept parked until the collection path is agreed.
